### 4. Sidebar long-menu — scrollbar vs chevrons
**User:** "when the screen height is not high enough to handle all of the menu items … a vertical scroll bar appears. Is this how most apps work with long menus? Or do most suppress the scroll bar in favor of chevrons for up and down movement much like we did for the Stepper"
**Claude:** Answered: vertical scroll is standard; chevrons are rare/worse for a *vertical* nav (the stepper got them because it scrolls *horizontally*, which is unexpected). Well-regarded apps (Slack/Discord/Linear/Notion) do a *refined* scroll: pin header + account, scroll the middle list, thin/auto-hiding bar. Offered A/B/C.

**User:** "A" (pin logo+profile, scroll middle, thin bar)
**Claude:** Restructured `Sidebar.tsx`: header (logo) + account row **pinned**, primary-nav region scrolls in the middle (`.sidebar-scroll`, thin auto-hiding bar). Utilities (Messages/Notifications/Admin/Mod) kept bottom-aligned via `mt-auto` **inside the scroll region** so the tall-screen look is unchanged. First cut pinned the *whole* bottom cluster (236px for a multi-role admin) which starved the scroll region on short screens → corrected to pin only the account row. **Retired the Conv-368 SIDEBAR-COLLIDE JS merge observer** (a real scroll region means the clusters can't collide); kept the `@media(max-height:500px)` compaction. Applied to expanded rail, collapsed 70px rail, and the shared mobile drawer. Verified: tall=unchanged, 420px→scroll region 234px usable & scrollable.

### 5. Horizontal scrollbar appeared with the vertical one
**User:** "Once the vertical scroll bar appears, a horizontal scrollbar also appears" [+ screenshot]
**Claude:** Root cause: styling `scrollbar-width: thin` makes Chrome render a **layout-taking ~11px** bar; the vertical bar ate 11px of width and the `w-full` rows overflowed → horizontal bar (the classic `overflow-y:auto` ⇒ computed `overflow-x:auto`). Added `overflow-x-hidden` to both scroll regions. Verified no horizontal bar, active pill not visibly clipped.

### 6. Scrollbar discoverability
**User:** "When does the scrollbar appear? Hover shows nothing, only click shows it. Can I scroll without clicking? Worry: this presentation doesn't indicate more items exist."
**Claude:** Clarified you CAN scroll on hover (trackpad/wheel) — the click only triggers `:focus-within` reveal (Chrome hover-reveal for a styled `scrollbar-color` is unreliable on macOS). The real gap is the missing affordance. Offered A/B/C.

**User:** "What do the apps you mentioned do with this config to show more options are available by scrolling?"
**Claude:** Honest correction to my earlier "always-visible bar is standard": those apps mostly **auto-hide too**; their "more" cues are the scrollbar flashing on scroll-gesture + **partial-clipping of the last row**. The ones adding an explicit signal use a **bottom fade / scroll-shadow** (Material's scrollable-drawer guidance). Re-offered A/B/C; leaned B.

**User:** "B … as long as it goes away at the top if you are at the first scrolled item, and similarly for the bottom"
**Claude:** Implemented `[SBAR-FADE]` first as a **CSS mask on the scroll viewport** with a JS scroll+resize listener toggling `data-fade-top`/`data-fade-bottom`. Verified TOP→bottom-fade only, MIDDLE→both, BOTTOM→top-fade only.

### 7. Fade too subtle → move to background
**User:** "Your mask is subtle. I only see it if text on the menu item is very near to the top or bottom edge … due to the large height of each button. Can we move the fade to the background instead of the text?"
**Claude:** The content-mask left the tall gaps between buttons blank. Replaced it with a **pure-CSS scroll-shadow on the region background** (Lea Verou technique: grey `background-attachment: local` "cover" gradients over dark `scroll`-attachment "shadow" gradients → shadow auto-shows at an edge only while items remain past it, gone at that end; **no JS**). Removed the JS listener + `data-fade` attrs + hook imports. Shows continuously in the gaps. Bumped to `rgba(15,23,42,0.22)/24px` for visibility.

**User:** "Could you decrease the shading by 30%?"
**Claude:** Opacity `0.22 → 0.154` (×0.7) on both edges; size unchanged.

**User:** "Good! A" → committed `d198368d` (sidebar long-menu, pin/scroll + horizontal-fix + scroll-shadow) + redeployed staging (Version `7470f20d`).

### 8. Wheel over the aside scrolled the main panel
**User:** "Is there a way to sense you are over the aside's region and capture the mouse scroll event? Most of the time scrolling over the aside scrolls the main content panel."
**Claude:** Diagnosed as **scroll-chaining** (wheel over the pinned header/footer, or at the region's scroll boundary, falls through to the page). Added `[SBAR-WHEEL]`: a `wheel` listener on the aside (callback ref, `passive:false`) forwards the vertical delta to `.sidebar-scroll` and `preventDefault`s, so wheel anywhere over the aside scrolls the nav — never main — unless the nav fits (page scrolls normally). Normalizes `deltaMode` (line/page), ignores ctrl (pinch-zoom). Added `overscroll-behavior: contain` as defense-in-depth. Verified: over header/region/footer → nav moves, page 0; at boundary → contained.

**User:** "B" → committed `4060d334` (not deployed).
