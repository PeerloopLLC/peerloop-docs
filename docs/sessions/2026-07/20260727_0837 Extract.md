### New Patterns
- **Counterfactual measurement** — to learn what a *removed* token had been rendering, re-apply its value to the live element and measure: `el.style.width='1.15em'` vs current. This is what turned "the rescind was a simplification" into "the rescind was a repair of a −14% shrink".
- **The invariant counter as codemod proof** — pick a number the change must not move (`dimension-bare-numeric` = 532) and check it before and after. One number rules out the whole wrong-conversion failure mode.
- **Informational rule tier in a baseline gate** — rules measured and reported but excluded from the headline total and regression check, stored under a separate `informational` key so a jump still shows in the diff.
