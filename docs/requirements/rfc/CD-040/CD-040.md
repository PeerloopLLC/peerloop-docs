## Purpose

Five client-requested changes accumulated across two working conversations. Each began the
same way: the client had already built the thing on his own branch (`brian-July-7`), and the
request was to adopt his version. Each had **previously been declined** in the MERGE-BRIAN
review — and in every case the recorded objection turned out to be about the *carrier*, not
the design.

That is the through-line of this batch, and it is the thing to say when walking the ledger
with him:

> **The objection was dissolved, not overruled.**

- His band blues were declined as **raw hex in a component** → adopted verbatim as named
  `--brian-band` / `--brian-band-line` tokens. The shipped colour is his; no raw hex entered
  a component.
- His pill shadow was declined for the same reason → adopted as `--brian-pill-shadow{,-hover}`.
  His shadow tint turned out to be `rgba(16,42,67)` = `#102A43` = **`--brian-ink`, a token
  already adopted from his tab palette**, so the elevation cost no new colour at all.
- His per-card CTA was declined because he derived it from **a snapshot blind to scheduled
  sessions** → adopted by routing the catalog through the detail page's own resolver, so the
  two surfaces cannot drift.

Five rows in [plan/merge-brian/NOT-ADOPTED.md](../../../../plan/merge-brian/NOT-ADOPTED.md)
were amended to record this (2 under § Communities, Conv 433; 3 under § Courses, Conv 434).
**Re-read those five rows before the client walkthrough** — they carry the exact wording.

## Decision 1: Community band colour + logo identity (Conv 433)

> "We need to make the background colour of the community section at the bottom edge of the
> card to be the clients, and the community logo to be the size, presentation and position
> the client used"

- Band blues adopted **verbatim** as `--brian-band` (`#e3f1fc`) / `--brian-band-line`
  (`#d3e7f8`), in the same `brian-*` provenance namespace as the already-adopted tab theme.
- Both catalog cards aligned on one band blue.
- The medallion (white-ringed seal rising above the band edge) adopted on the **`/courses`
  cover-story card** — his size, position and white ring.

**Decision 1a — the medallion is deliberately NOT on the community catalog card.** This was
the client's own question ("should that be the logo instead of the image?"), and the answer
turns on what the asset *means* in each place:

| Surface | What the logo is | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| `/courses` cover-story card | Names **another** community — a cross-reference | Medallion bridges the cover→band seam |
| Community catalog card | The card's **own** subject | Leads with the logo forward (56px squircle) over a scrimmed cover — no seam to bridge |

The forward-squircle treatment matches what `/community/[slug]`'s hero and the client's own
community card already do, so this is alignment rather than divergence.

**Open consequence:** community art is still all `picsum` placeholders. `cover_image_url`
has a UI slot but **no upload and no storage** — tracked as `[COMM-IMG]`.

## Decision 2: Topic-pill hover elevation (Conv 434)

> "the client wants the hover to not colour the buttons but instead to make the pills rise
> up slightly. There needs to be a dropshadow on each pill and that is deeper when the pill
> is hovered because the button lifts up on hover."

- Two-layer resting shadow + deeper hover shadow carried as `--brian-pill-shadow` /
  `--brian-pill-shadow-hover`, with an `@theme` bridge.
- `hover:bg-neutral-100` removed — hover no longer tints the capsule, per the request.
- 1px hover lift, with a `motion-reduce` guard.
- The scroller's `py-2` → `py-12`, because `overflow-x-auto` computes `overflow-y: auto` and
  was clipping the new shadow.

**Decision 2a — scope kept LOCAL to that pill row, by user decision.** "I don't want to make
the shadow site-wide on pills yet." The app-wide `--shadow-*` scale is inert and needs a
deliberate pass; that is parked as `[SHADOW-DEAD]` and is explicitly **not** to be picked up
as a token cleanup.

The gradient selected-capsule and `#2a93d5` from his branch stay behind.

## Decision 3: Per-card journey CTA (Conv 434)

> "'Book next session' for when you have paid for a course, have taken at least 1 session and
> have not taken all the sessions; 'Book first session' when you have paid but not taken any
> sessions; 'Enroll Now' for courses you have never purchased; and 'Teach this course' for a
> course you have successfully completed."

Adopted, with one addition and one structural change.

**The structural change is the whole point.** `buildCoursePrimaryCta` moved into
`@lib/course-cta` and is now **the only place a course CTA is decided**. Three surfaces call
it — `/courses`, `/course/[slug]`, and the community Courses tab. A parity test
(`tests/lib/course-cta.test.ts`) exists to fail if a fourth implementation appears.

Feeding it correctly mattered as much as calling it: `nextSessionId` was added to the
`/api/me/full` snapshot with a predicate **deliberately matched to `computeCourseJourney`**
(scheduled OR in_progress, no future-only filter). The existing `nextSessionAt` answers a
different question and would have re-created the very divergence that got the client's
version declined.

**The addition — a fifth state his spec omits.** His four states have no slot for "you
already have a session booked". Without it, a student with session 3 booked reads "Book next
session" on the catalog and "Go to Session 3" one click later. Verified on the exact student
who would have shown the bug: catalog and detail now both read "Go to Session 2".

**"Teach this course" points at the student's Diploma** — see Decision 4, which is the flow
that grew out of this gap.

### Decision 3a: three of OUR defects, exposed by the client's request

This is the batch's strongest argument and should not be buried as internal cleanup. One
conflated identifier — `myCourseIds` — was suppressing the enrol CTA for four unrelated
relationships at once:

| Defect | Symptom |
|---|---|
| `[CTA-MOD-GAP]` | A community **moderator** saw blank cards |
| `[CTA-TEACHER-DUP]` | A **certified teacher** was told to "Teach this course" |
| (same fix) | A certified teacher who never enrolled got **nothing at all** |
| `[CTA-CANCELLED]` | **Cancelled** enrolments disagreed between surfaces |

`myCourseIds` now means creators only; everything else routes through the shared resolver.
The teacher rule was put **in the resolver** rather than at the call site, so both surfaces
pass `isTeacherOfCourse` and no fresh divergence was introduced while fixing the old one.

`[CARD-CTA-COMM]`: the community Courses tab was found still running its own stale logic —
enrolled viewers got no CTA at all there. Wired to the shared resolver, preserving `?via=`
attribution on course-bound hrefs only.

## Decision 4: Student request-to-teach flow (Conv 434)

> "We had discussed wiring in the message to the creator of the course and sending the
> details of the course and student so that the creator can start the process as only they
> can. We also discussed a record of the message being sent, so that the next time that
> student sees the course in the listing or on its detail page that 'Request has been sent'
> displays."

Peerloop's flywheel ends with "complete a course → teach it", but until this change a
student had no way to *start* that: `POST /api/me/certificates/recommend` must be called by
someone vouching **for** them, never by themselves. So "Teach this course" pointed at the
student's diploma and stopped.

- `enrollments.teaching_request_sent_at` — the stamp that flips the CTA to "Request sent" on
  all three surfaces.
- `POST /api/me/courses/[courseId]/teaching-request` — **idempotent**: a replay returns 200
  `alreadySent` and does not message the creator again. The stamp is the guard; without it the
  CTA is a spam button aimed at a real inbox.
- `/course/[slug]/teach` — the authenticated request page.

**Decision 4a — the creator needs somewhere to act.** `/creating/requests` (a new Requests
tab in the creator workspace) plus `GET /api/me/teaching-requests`, scoped by **authorship**
rather than by teaching certification.

That scoping is not incidental: a creator usually holds **no teaching certification for their
own course** (seed data: 4 of Guy's 4 do, 0 of Gabriel's 2 do), which is exactly why the
teacher-gated workspace was the wrong home for this action.

**Decision 4b — a documented model/code contradiction, resolved in favour of the docs.**
CLAUDE.md has always said creators certify teachers; `recommend.ts` said teacher-only. It was
widened to accept the course creator. Note there were **two** authorization gates, not one —
a positive test caught the second, which would otherwise have left the fix inert.

## Decision 5: A defect class that was invisible to every gate

Surfaced while building the above: **invented Tailwind token names and MattIcon names fail
silently.** `npm run check:tokens` (`scripts/check-token-names.ts`) now catches them, and
found **6 pre-existing `text-warning-600/700` defects** on its first run — a scale that only
goes 100/300/500. It polices only project-owned families, so false positives stay at zero.

## Implied user stories

- As a **visitor browsing `/courses`**, I can see at a glance what my next step is on every
  course, without opening it.
- As a **student who completed a course**, I can ask its creator to certify me to teach it,
  and see that my request was sent.
- As a **creator**, I have one place that lists everyone who has asked to teach my courses,
  and I can act on each without holding a teaching certification myself.
- As a **community moderator or certified teacher**, the course catalog shows me an action
  appropriate to my relationship with the course, not a blank space.
